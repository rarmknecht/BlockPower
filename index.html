How to Play

Slide the blocking vehicles along their lanes to clear a path for the Red Jeep to escape through the EXIT gate on the right wall.

  • Red Jeep (2 spaces): Your escape vehicle, and the only one that's always red. Slide it out the exit!
  • Jeeps (2 spaces): Compact 4×4s with a spare tire on the back.
  • Residential Trucks (2 or 3 spaces): Pickups and utility vans with an open cargo bed.
  • Commercial Trucks (3 spaces): Freight haulers with a ribbed cargo box.

Every other vehicle gets a random paint job, so tell them apart by shape and length, not color.

Touch / mouse: Drag any vehicle along its lane.

Keyboard:

  • Tab / Shift+Tab Select the next / previous vehicle
  • Arrows or WASD Slide the selected vehicle one space
  • Shift+Arrow Slide as far as it will go
  • H Hint   U or Ctrl+Z Undo   Esc Close dialogs

Consecutive keyboard slides of the same vehicle count as a single move.
